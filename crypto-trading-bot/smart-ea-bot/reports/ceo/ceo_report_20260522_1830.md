## I. CRITICAL FINDINGS

1. **Data limitation:** Alpaca 5-min crypto history is only ~5 weeks. This is too short for robust backtesting. Need longer history.

2. **Crypto regime bias:** The Feb-Mar 2026 period was directional (trending_down dominant). Mean reversion strategies naturally suffer in this regime.

4. **Stop-loss sizing is critical:** Tight stops (0.3%) get hit by noise. Wide stops (1.5×ATR) let losses run. Need dynamic, regime-aware sizing.

5. **Simple may be better:** Complex multi-indicator strategies are failing. A simple "trend following with regime filter" may outperform.
